# Nestle-FMCGNestlé FMCG Australia – Sales & Revenue Dashboard | Power BI

 Project Overview

The Nestlé FMCG Australia Sales & Revenue Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze sales performance, revenue trends, product performance, and sales distribution across different locations and sales channels in Australia.

The dashboard helps businesses understand monthly revenue trends, identify top-performing products, evaluate sales channels, and analyze geographical sales performance.

The analysis covers the period from 1 September 2018 to 30 November 2020.

 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Monitor monthly revenue trends.
- Identify top-performing products based on revenue.
- Compare revenue generated through Direct and Online sales channels.
- Analyze sales performance across Australian states and territories.
- Provide insights into product performance.
- Support business decisions through interactive visualizations.

 Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query (data preparation, where applicable)
- DAX (measures and calculations, where applicable)
- Data Visualization

 Dashboard Features

1. Revenue by Month

Analyzes monthly revenue trends and helps identify changes in business performance throughout the year.

2. Revenue by Product Name

Identifies the products contributing the highest revenue.

Top-performing products displayed include:

- Milo – 6.0M
- Nescafe – 5.5M
- Nesquik Duo – 4.3M
- Nes Cau – 3.3M
- Nestlé Drumstick – 3.2M
- Smarties – 2.4M
- Kit Kat – 2.2M
- Maggi – 2.2M
- Nescafe Gold – 1.7M

3. Revenue by Sales Medium

Compares revenue generated through two sales channels:

- Direct Sales – 23.8M (77.24%)
- Online Sales – 7.02M (22.76%)

4. Revenue by Sales Location

Analyzes revenue distribution across Australian states and territories, including:

- South Australia
- Tasmania
- Queensland
- New South Wales
- Northern Territory
- Victoria
- Australian Capital Territory
- Western Australia

5. Sales Location Analysis

Provides a geographical breakdown of revenue to help identify regional sales performance.

6. Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive slicers for:
- Month
- Product Name
- Sales Medium

These filters allow users to explore the data from different business perspectives.
Insights

- The dashboard displays total revenue of 30.82M.
- Total sales and transactions are displayed as 18K each.
- Milo is the highest revenue-generating product, contributing approximately 6.0M.
- Nescafe is the second-highest revenue-generating product, contributing approximately 5.5M.
- Direct sales contribute 77.24% of total revenue.
- Online sales contribute 22.76% of total revenue.
- Revenue by location helps identify differences in regional sales performance.
- Monthly revenue analysis highlights changes in sales performance over time.

Business Recommendations

- Strengthen online sales strategies to increase the online revenue contribution.
- Focus on high-performing products such as Milo and Nescafe.
- Analyze low-performing products to identify opportunities for improvement.
- Develop location-specific marketing strategies.
- Monitor monthly revenue trends to support better sales forecasting.
- Improve sales channel performance through data-driven business decisions.

 Conclusion
This project demonstrates the use of Power BI for FMCG sales and revenue analysis.
The dashboard transforms sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, KPI tracking, product analysis, monthly revenue trends, and geographical sales analysis.
It helps businesses understand sales performance, identify revenue opportunities, and make informed strategic decisions.
